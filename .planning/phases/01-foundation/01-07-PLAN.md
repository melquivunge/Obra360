Close gap G-01 (full Portuguese throughout) and the remaining slice of G-04 (auth screen identity) by eliminating every English Breeze surface that is still reachable in the app.

Investigation found that the primary auth screens (login, forgot-password, reset-password, confirm-password), both layout shells, both dashboards, and the user-management CRUD are already Portuguese and styled.
